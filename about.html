Meet Joshua

Who am I?

I'm a software engineer who seeks to provide value for people with AI.

I work hard and I'm driven by the idea that we can harness the power of AI to make people's lives better.

How do you work with others?

I'd rather empower a teammate than optimize for short-term speed.

A newly joined engineer was struggling on work I didn't have bandwidth for. It would've been faster to do it myself, but I guided him instead, pointing him in the right direction while letting him own the task. He finished, gained confidence, and our trust grew. That's the team I want to be a part of: patient, supportive, and aligned around a shared goal.

What kind of problems do you love?

I'm drawn to complex problems where the solution depends on understanding how many moving pieces interact. In everyday life, that might mean figuring out how to get 7 people to dinner in 30 minutes, 15 miles away, across 2 cars, when one car is low on gas. In software engineering, it means designing systems that scale to thousands of users, handle real-time data, and balance constraints like performance, reliability, and cost.

How do you handle setbacks?

I don't call it wasted work, I call it learning

I learn from mistakes, and solving the problem is the priority, not defending your first approach. Once, I spent days on a solution that turned out not to be the right fit, but I didn't call it wasted work, I called it learning. Pivoting is part of the process.
