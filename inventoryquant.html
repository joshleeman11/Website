InventoryQuant

Y Combinator W26 · Applied AI Software Engineer · 2025–2026

What was the problem?

Public insurance adjusters were spending days on every claim manually transcribing line items from loss-site walkthroughs, hunting replacement prices across retailers, and formatting submission-ready reports. The work was repetitive, error-prone, and didn't scale as claim volume grew.

What did I build to solve it?

I owned AI-powered systems across the full platform: record a video walkthrough, and InventoryQuant transcribes every line item, matches replacement prices from major retailers, and exports XactContents-ready reports. I mapped the end-to-end pipeline—capture, transcription, pricing, export—before building, and shipped production systems that cut work from 4 days to 4 hours per claim. That included prompt engineering, an LLM fine-tuning and eval pipeline, and full-stack features in Next.js and Python.
