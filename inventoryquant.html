What was the problem?

Public insurance adjusters take inventory of all items to be filed for insurance, but this process was too time-consuming. It typically took days on every claim to jot down every line item, find replacement prices across retailers, and format a submission-ready report. It was repetitive, error-prone, and didn't scale as claim volume grew.

What did we build to solve it?

We realized the entire process could be automated. Our product ingests a user uploaded recording of their loss-site walkthrough, transcribes and structures each line item into an industry specific format, finds replacement prices from major retailers, and exports reports ready for submission to the insurance company.

4 days → 4 hours per claim
