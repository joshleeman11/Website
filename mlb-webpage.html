MLB Cache Management Tool

Major League Baseball · Software Engineer Intern · 2023

What was the problem?

When a player hits a home run, fans expect stats to update immediately on MLB.com. But cached data can't update while it's cached—so the Data Platform team needed a reliable internal tool to clear the right cache entries at the right time, without breaking anything during a live game.

What did I build to solve it?

I redesigned the internal NORAD Cache Jobs website for speed and safety. Using HTML, JavaScript, and React, I organized inputs into required vs. optional parameters, added validation against internal MLB APIs (sportId, leagueId, etc.), built a bulk Excel upload feature, and used React state to block invalid submissions before they hit production.
