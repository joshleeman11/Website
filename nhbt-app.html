NHBT Tournament App

National Hillel Basketball Tournament · Software Engineer · 2024

What was the problem?

A national basketball tournament with dozens of teams needed one place for players, coaches, and families to follow live scores, brackets, standings, and stats—without refreshing a clunky spreadsheet or asking someone at the scorer's table.

What did I build to solve it?

My teammate and I built the full app in Flutter, wired it to a Firebase backend for real-time updates, and stayed in constant contact with game operations to match tournament rules—tier structures, stat tracking, half-based games, and more. In March 2024, over 1,000 people used the app across the tournament weekend on iOS and Android.
