National Hillel Basketball Tournament · Software Engineer · 2024

View on the App Store →

What was the problem?

A collegiate basketball tournament that has expanded to include 50+ schools and 500+ players needed an app to follow live scores, brackets, standings, and stats

What did we build to solve it?

We built a Firebase backend for real-time updates, a Flutter mobile app for users to follow live, and a React web app for admins to manage the tournament and update scores and stats. We stayed in constant contact with game operations to align with tournament rules. In March 2024, 1,000+ people used the app across the tournament weekend.
